# 📁 Extended UI/UX Portfolio Projects (12 Total)

This portfolio covers a wide range of industries and design challenges.

### 1. EcoStream (Environment)
*   **Challenge:** Visualizing complex carbon data.
*   **Key Outcome:** Glassmorphism dashboard with clear hierarchy.

### 2. NeoBank (Fintech)
*   **Challenge:** Making banking feel "modern" and "fun" for Gen-Z.
*   **Key Outcome:** High-contrast neon aesthetics and minimal interaction paths.

### 3. MediConnect (Healthcare)
*   **Focus:** Accessibility and trust. Soft color palettes and large click targets.

### 4. Wanderlust (Travel)
*   **Focus:** Storytelling through high-resolution imagery and smooth transitions.

### 5. FitTrack (Fitness)
*   **Focus:** Gamification. Progress rings and social competition modules.

### 6. FoodieExpress (Delivery)
*   **Focus:** Efficiency. One-tap reordering and real-time map tracking.

### 7. EduPulse (EdTech)
*   **Focus:** Focus-mode UI. Minimizing distractions during study sessions.

### 8. LuxStay (Real Estate)
*   **Focus:** Premium feel. Elegant serif typography and minimalist listings.

### 9. CryptoNode (Blockchain)
*   **Focus:** Real-time data. Live price tickers and candlestick chart integration.

### 10. SmartHome (IoT)
*   **Focus:** Control center. Toggle switches and haptic feedback design.

### 11. EventHive (Social)
*   **Focus:** Community. Calendar views and RSVP management.

### 12. DevPort (Portfolio Template)
*   **Focus:** Self-branding. Clean grid for developers to showcase work.
